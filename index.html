Clovis

A Destiny 2 raid and dungeon scheduling bot for Discord.

  • Weekly availability polls where everyone picks their days, times and activities
  • Best-night suggestions with a start time that fits the most people
  • Rosters with waitlists, reminders, Discord events and encounter-role boards
  • Featured raid and dungeon tags, raid and dungeon exotic tracking, and post-run recaps
